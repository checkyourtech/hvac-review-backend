- No blower speed verification documented.
- No evaporator coil cleanliness inspection documented.
- No duct restriction inspection documented.
- No superheat measurement documented.
- No subcooling measurement documented.

Add 3 pounds of refrigerant.

Scope of work:
- Add refrigerant
- Test cooling operation
