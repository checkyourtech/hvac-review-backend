HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner is running but cooling capacity is poor.

Diagnosis:
Restricted TXV.

Diagnostic findings:
- Indoor airflow verified and within expected range.
- Air filter is clean.
- Evaporator coil is clean.
- Indoor blower is operating properly.
- Refrigerant charge verified before condemning the TXV.
- Suction pressure is low.
- Superheat is high.
- Subcooling is normal.
- Liquid line temperature was checked.
- No abnormal temperature drop was found across the filter drier.
- Filter drier does not appear restricted.
- TXV sensing bulb is properly mounted and insulated.
- External equalizer line is properly connected and unobstructed.
- Adequate liquid refrigerant is reaching the TXV inlet.
- TXV does not respond properly to changing load/bulb temperature.
- No other liquid-line restriction was found.

Recommended repair:
Replace restricted TXV.

Scope of work:
- Recover refrigerant
- Replace TXV
- Replace liquid-line filter drier
- Nitrogen pressure test
- Evacuate system
- Recharge to manufacturer specifications
- Verify superheat and subcooling
- Confirm proper cooling operation

Total repair price: $2,650

Parts and labor included.

1-year parts and labor warranty.
