Customer reports poor cooling.

System inspected and found low on R-410A refrigerant.

Electronic leak detection identified a refrigerant leak at the evaporator coil. Leak location was confirmed with soap bubbles at the coil.

Recommended repair:
Replace evaporator coil.

Scope includes:
- new OEM-compatible evaporator coil
- new liquid-line filter drier
- recover remaining refrigerant
- remove and replace evaporator coil
- nitrogen pressure test after repair
- evacuate system to 500 microns or less
- verify vacuum holds before charging
- recharge system with R-410A
- verify system operation after repair

- 2-year labor warranty
