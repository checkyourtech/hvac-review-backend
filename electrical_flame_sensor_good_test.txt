HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Gas furnace ignites normally, but the burners shut off after a few seconds and the furnace retries.

Diagnosis:
Failed flame sensor.

Diagnostic findings:
- Furnace ignition sequence verified.
- Burners ignite normally.
- Flame sensor is positioned in the burner flame.
- Flame sensor wiring and connections inspected and found secure.
- Furnace grounding inspected and verified.
- Burner flame appears stable and properly contacts the flame sensor.
- Flame signal measured at 0.4 microamps DC.
- Manufacturer minimum flame signal is 1.5 microamps DC.
- Flame sensor was cleaned and retested.
- Flame signal remained at 0.4 microamps DC after cleaning.
- Control board continues to shut the burners down due to loss of flame-proving signal.
- No other wiring, grounding, or burner-flame condition was found that would explain the weak flame signal.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed flame sensor.

Scope of work:
- Remove failed flame sensor
- Install replacement flame sensor
- Verify wiring and electrical connections
- Verify proper flame sensor positioning
- Measure flame signal after repair
- Operate furnace through a complete heating cycle
- Confirm normal furnace operation
