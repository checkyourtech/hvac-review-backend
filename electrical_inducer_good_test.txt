HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Gas furnace will not heat. Inducer does not start on a call for heat.

Diagnosis:
Failed draft inducer motor.

Diagnostic findings:
- 120 volts measured at the inducer motor during a call for heat.
- Control board output to the inducer verified.
- Inducer wiring and connectors inspected and found secure.
- Inducer wheel checked and is not obstructed.
- Motor receives proper voltage but does not start.
- Motor winding resistance tested outside expected range.
- Pressure switch tubing and venting inspected with no blockage found.
- No condensate restriction found.
- Furnace fault history reviewed and no other fault was identified that would prevent inducer operation.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed draft inducer motor assembly.

Scope of work:
- Remove failed inducer assembly
- Install replacement inducer assembly
- Replace inducer gasket/seal as needed
- Verify wiring and electrical connections
- Verify inducer starts properly on a call for heat
- Confirm pressure switch proves draft
- Operate furnace through a complete heating cycle
- Confirm normal furnace operation

Parts and labor included.
