HVAC SYSTEM REPLACEMENT PROPOSAL
Project: Sample Home D, 100 Example Lane. Whole conditioned home, single system.
The homeowner requests a planned replacement of functioning equipment during renovation.
No failure is claimed. Replace the complete existing HVAC system.

Submitted equipment matching:
The attached manufacturer combination sheet D-17 lists the exact proposed outdoor unit,
indoor unit and metering device together as an approved combination. The attached AHRI
reference D-17 identifies the same models and refrigerant. All equipment uses the documented
208/230V single-phase supply. Matching is documented separately from building sizing.

Proposed equipment: Example HP36 heat pump with Example AH36 air handler and Example EH10 supplemental heat.
Nominal cooling capacity: 3 refrigeration tons.
Submitted building-specific load/selection summary D-17 for Sample Home D, whole home:
Cooling load: 34,000 Btu/h. Heating load: 48,000 Btu/h.
Outdoor design temperatures: 95 F cooling and 5 F heating.
Indoor design temperatures: 75 F cooling and 70 F heating.
The supplied manufacturer data gives 35,000 Btu/h cooling at the stated cooling conditions
and 28,000 Btu/h heat-pump heating output at the stated heating design conditions.
The submitted design intentionally uses supplemental electric heat with documented
available output of 34,120 Btu/h and staged controls permitting simultaneous operation.
The design documents backup coverage of the 20,000 Btu/h difference at heating design conditions.
This building-specific selection supports the heat pump plus supplemental heat strategy.
It does not require the heat pump alone to meet the whole heating load.

Installation scope:
Remove existing equipment and install the listed indoor and outdoor units. The proposal
includes manufacturer-specified installation, permits, startup and verification of heating
and cooling operation. Submitted duct review supports the specified equipment airflow.
10-year parts warranty and 2-year labor warranty are included.

Total installed price: $18,000.
Equipment: $11,000.
Labor and installation materials: $7,000.
