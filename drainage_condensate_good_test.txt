HVAC SERVICE PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Water leaking around indoor air handler.

Equipment:
Residential split-system air conditioner
Upflow air handler

Diagnostic findings:

- Water observed leaking from the air handler during cooling operation.
- Evaporator coil inspected and found clean with no evidence of icing.
- Blower operation verified normal.
- Condensate drain line inspected and found heavily restricted with biological buildup.
- Drain trap was clogged and holding water.
- Primary drain pan visually inspected and found intact with no cracks or damage.
- Secondary drain pan inspected and found dry and undamaged.
- Condensate pump tested and operating normally.
- Drain line was disconnected and flushed with water and nitrogen.
- Drain trap was cleaned.
- After cleaning, water was poured into the evaporator drain pan and flowed freely through the drain line.
- System was operated in cooling for 30 minutes after repair.
- No additional water leakage was observed.
- Evaporator condensate drained normally during operation.

Diagnosis:
Clogged condensate drain line and drain trap causing water to back up into the air handler.

Recommended repair:
Clean and flush condensate drain system.

Scope of work:

- Clear primary condensate drain line
- Clean drain trap
- Flush drain piping
- Reassemble drain system
- Verify proper drainage
- Run system and confirm no additional leakage

Total repair price:
$425

Warranty:
90-day labor warranty on condensate drain cleaning.

Technician notes:
Evaporator coil replacement and drain pan replacement are not recommended. The coil and drain pans were inspected and showed no evidence of leakage or physical damage.
