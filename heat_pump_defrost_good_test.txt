HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Heat pump is running in heating mode but supply air remains cool.

Diagnosis:
Failed reversing valve.

Diagnostic findings:
- Thermostat is configured correctly for the equipment O/B logic.
- Thermostat is calling for heating.
- Reversing-valve solenoid voltage verified at 24 VAC during the required operating mode.
- Reversing-valve coil resistance tested and is within manufacturer specifications.
- Reversing-valve solenoid energizes correctly.
- Outdoor unit and indoor blower are operating.
- Airflow verified normal.
- Air filter and indoor coil are clean.
- Refrigerant charge checked and found within manufacturer requirements.
- Suction and discharge pressures measured during heating operation.
- Refrigerant line temperatures measured at the reversing valve.
- Temperature readings indicate the valve is not fully redirecting refrigerant flow.
- Significant temperature mixing is present across the reversing valve.
- Defrost board operation checked and found normal.
- Outdoor temperature sensor tested and found within specification.
- No active defrost condition is present.
- Valve was commanded between heating and cooling modes and failed to shift completely.
- Diagnosis confirms an internally leaking or mechanically stuck reversing valve.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed reversing valve.

Scope of work:
- Recover refrigerant
- Remove failed reversing valve
- Install replacement reversing valve
- Replace filter drier
- Nitrogen purge while brazing
- Pressure test refrigerant circuit with nitrogen
- Evacuate system to proper vacuum level
- Recharge system according to manufacturer requirements
- Verify refrigerant charge
- Verify reversing-valve operation in heating and cooling
- Verify system pressures and temperatures
- Test defrost operation
- Run system through complete heating cycle
