HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Gas furnace will not heat. Inducer motor starts, but ignition sequence stops before ignition.

Diagnosis:
Failed furnace pressure switch.

Diagnostic findings:
- Draft inducer motor starts and operates normally on a call for heat.
- Inducer motor voltage and operation verified.
- Pressure switch tubing and pressure ports inspected and found clear and secure.
- Venting and combustion-air path inspected with no blockage found.
- Condensate drain and trap inspected with no restriction found.
- Draft pressure measured at -1.75 in. w.c.
- Pressure switch is rated to close at -0.90 in. w.c.
- Pressure switch does not close even though measured draft exceeds its required closing pressure.
- Pressure switch continuity was tested and switch remains open under proper draft.
- Wiring and electrical connections to the pressure switch were inspected and found secure.
- No other condition was found that would prevent proper pressure-switch operation.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed furnace pressure switch.

Scope of work:
- Remove existing pressure switch
- Install replacement pressure switch
- Reconnect tubing and wiring
- Verify pressure switch proves draft
- Operate furnace through a complete heating cycle
- Confirm normal furnace operation
