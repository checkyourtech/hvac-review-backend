Customer reports outdoor unit will not start.

Technician inspected system and found a failed dual run capacitor and badly pitted contactor.

Measured capacitor:
45/5 µF rated
Compressor side measured 18 µF
Fan side measured 2.1 µF

Contactor contacts are visibly burned and pitted.

Recommended repair:
- replace dual run capacitor
- replace contactor
- inspect wiring and terminals
- verify proper voltage after repair
- verify compressor and condenser fan operation

Total repair price: $725
