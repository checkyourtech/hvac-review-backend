HVAC REPLACEMENT PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Furnace is heating, but the technician observed abnormal burner operation during an inspection.

Diagnosis:
Suspected heat-exchanger failure. Furnace replacement is recommended.

Diagnostic findings:
- Furnace is approximately 16 years old.
- Burner flames move noticeably when the circulating blower starts.
- A borescope inspection identified a discolored and irregular area near a heat-exchanger seam.
- The proposal does not document a clearly visible crack, hole, split, or separation.
- No photograph or borescope image identifying a failed opening is included.
- Flue-gas carbon monoxide measured at 165 ppm air-free during operation.
- The proposal does not identify a manufacturer rejection criterion met by the observed area.
- Gas manifold pressure was checked and reported within the manufacturer's range.
- Venting and inducer operation were inspected with no obstruction documented.
- The contractor recommends additional safety concern be resolved through furnace replacement.

Recommended work:
Replace the furnace because of the suspected heat-exchanger condition.

Scope of work:
- Remove existing furnace
- Install replacement furnace
- Reconnect gas piping, venting, electrical, controls, and ductwork
- Verify gas manifold pressure
- Perform combustion analysis
- Check carbon monoxide after installation
- Run the furnace through a complete heating cycle

Total replacement price: $9,850

Parts and labor included.

10-year manufacturer parts warranty.
2-year labor warranty.
