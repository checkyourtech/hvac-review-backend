HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner is running but not cooling properly.

Diagnosis:
System was inspected and indoor airflow was verified before evaluating refrigerant charge.

Air filter is clean.
Evaporator coil is clean.
Indoor blower is operating properly.
Supply and return airflow are unobstructed.

Outdoor ambient temperature: 92°F
Indoor return-air temperature: 76°F

System uses R-410A refrigerant.

Technician measured:
Suction pressure: 102 PSIG
Liquid pressure: 318 PSIG
Superheat: 29°F
Subcooling: 2°F

Manufacturer target subcooling under current operating conditions: 10°F.

Technician determined that the measured operating conditions are consistent with an undercharged system after airflow conditions were verified.

Electronic leak detection was performed at accessible refrigerant piping, service valves, indoor coil connections, and outdoor coil connections.

Leak detected at the liquid-line service valve Schrader core.

Recommended repair:
- Recover refrigerant as necessary
- Replace leaking Schrader core
- Install new valve cap/seal
- Pressure test repaired connection with nitrogen
- Evacuate system as required
- Recharge system with R-410A to manufacturer specifications
- Verify final refrigerant charge using manufacturer charging procedure
- Confirm proper superheat/subcooling after repair
- Verify system cooling operation

Repair includes refrigerant, materials, and labor.

Total repair price: $875

1-year parts and labor warranty on repair.
