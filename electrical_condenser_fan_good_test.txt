HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner runs but shuts off on high pressure after several minutes.

Diagnosis:
Failed condenser fan motor.

Diagnostic findings:
- Outdoor condenser fan is not running during a cooling call.
- 240 VAC measured at condenser fan motor during call for cooling.
- Run capacitor tested at 7.4 µF against a rated 7.5 µF and is within tolerance.
- Fan blade spins freely with power disconnected.
- No mechanical obstruction found.
- Motor windings tested and show abnormal resistance.
- Motor tested to ground and no short to ground was found.
- Motor draws excessive amperage when attempting to start.
- Contactor is supplying proper voltage to the motor.
- Wiring and connections inspected and found secure.
- Condenser coil is clean and unrestricted.
- High-pressure switch opens after condenser temperature and pressure rise due to loss of condenser airflow.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed condenser fan motor.

Scope of work:
- Disconnect electrical power
- Remove failed condenser fan motor
- Install replacement condenser fan motor
- Reinstall fan blade and verify proper rotation
- Verify capacitor compatibility with replacement motor
- Restore power
- Verify condenser fan operation
- Check motor amperage
- Verify system pressures
- Run system through complete cooling cycle
