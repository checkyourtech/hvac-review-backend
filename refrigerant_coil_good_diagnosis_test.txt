HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner is running but not cooling well.

Diagnosis:
Evaporator coil is leaking refrigerant and requires replacement.

Diagnostic findings:
- Indoor airflow was checked and found acceptable.
- Air filter is clean.
- Evaporator coil is clean and not airflow-restricted.
- Indoor blower is operating properly.
- System refrigerant charge was found low.
- Electronic leak detector indicated refrigerant at the evaporator coil.
- Soap bubbles confirmed leakage at the evaporator coil tubing.
- No leak was found at accessible service valves, line-set connections, or outdoor coil.
- Leak location was confirmed at the evaporator coil.

Recommended repair:
Replace leaking evaporator coil.

Scope of work:
- Recover refrigerant
- Remove leaking evaporator coil
- Install replacement evaporator coil
- Replace liquid-line filter drier
- Pressure test repaired refrigerant circuit with nitrogen
- Evacuate system to 500 microns or lower
- Recharge system with manufacturer-specified refrigerant
- Verify proper refrigerant charge
- Verify system cooling operation

Total repair price: $4,850

Parts and labor included.

10-year manufacturer parts warranty on evaporator coil.
2-year labor warranty.
