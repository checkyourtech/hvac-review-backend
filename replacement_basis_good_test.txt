HVAC SYSTEM REPLACEMENT PROPOSAL

Existing system:
- 17-year-old split air-conditioning system
- Existing compressor is out of the major equipment warranty
- Two major repairs were completed during the last three years

Documented findings:
- Correct voltage reaches the compressor
- Run capacitor tested within its rated tolerance
- Compressor windings were tested and continuity to ground was documented
- The compressor was diagnosed as electrically grounded

Options provided:
- Replace the compressor, filter drier, and refrigerant for $6,900
- Replace the complete system
- Because of the confirmed compressor failure, prior major repairs, age, and repair cost,
  the contractor recommends replacement as the better long-term option

Proposed replacement equipment:
- Outdoor heat pump: Northstar NHP36-24V, R-454B, 208/230V single phase
- Indoor air handler: Northstar NAH36-24V, R-454B, 208/230V single phase
- Factory metering device: Northstar TXV-36B

Submitted matched-system documentation:
- AHRI Certificate Reference 214567890 lists outdoor model NHP36-24V and indoor model NAH36-24V
- The certificate rates this exact combination at 16.0 SEER2, 12.0 EER2, and 8.1 HSPF2
- The submitted manufacturer combination sheet lists the same exact models with TXV-36B

Replacement scope:
- Remove the existing system
- Install the listed matched indoor and outdoor equipment
- Complete startup and verify heating and cooling operation

Total replacement price: $17,800
Equipment: $10,800
Labor and installation materials: $7,000
