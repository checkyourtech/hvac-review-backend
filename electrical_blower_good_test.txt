HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Indoor blower is not running. System is not moving air in heating or cooling.

Diagnosis:
Failed ECM blower motor/module.

Diagnostic findings:
- 240 volts measured at the blower motor power input.
- 24-volt blower command verified from the control board during a fan call.
- Blower harness and connectors inspected and found secure with no visible damage.
- Blower wheel spins freely by hand with no obstruction or seized bearings.
- Manufacturer diagnostic procedure performed.
- ECM motor/module receives proper power and control command but does not operate.
- Control board output verified normal.
- No other electrical faults found during testing.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed ECM blower motor/module with OEM-compatible replacement.

Scope of work:
- Remove failed ECM blower motor/module
- Install compatible replacement motor/module
- Verify wiring and connectors
- Configure blower speed/airflow settings as required
- Verify blower operation in heating, cooling, and fan-only modes
- Confirm normal system operation after repair

Parts and labor included.
