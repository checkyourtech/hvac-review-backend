HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Furnace is operating, but technician identified a safety concern during inspection.

Diagnosis:
Failed/cracked heat exchanger requiring furnace replacement.

Diagnostic findings:
- Furnace is approximately 15 years old.
- Heat exchanger inspected visually and with a borescope.
- Crack approximately 2 inches long documented in the primary heat exchanger near the burner inlet.
- Photograph of the crack was provided to the homeowner.
- Burner flame becomes visibly disturbed when the circulating blower starts.
- Combustion analysis performed.
- Flue-gas carbon monoxide measured at 280 ppm air-free.
- Ambient carbon monoxide near the supply registers increased during furnace operation.
- Gas manifold pressure verified within manufacturer specifications.
- Temperature rise checked and found within manufacturer range.
- Venting inspected and found unobstructed.
- Burner and inducer operation verified.
- Heat exchanger failure was confirmed before replacement was recommended.
- Technician advised discontinuing furnace operation due to combustion-safety concerns.

Recommended repair:
Replace existing furnace due to confirmed failed heat exchanger.

Scope of work:
- Remove existing furnace
- Install properly sized replacement furnace
- Connect existing gas piping as appropriate
- Connect venting
- Connect electrical and controls
- Connect supply and return ductwork
- Verify gas manifold pressure
- Verify temperature rise
- Perform combustion analysis
- Check for carbon monoxide
- Verify venting and inducer operation
- Run furnace through complete heating cycle
- Confirm safe operation after installation

Total replacement price: $9,850

Parts and labor included.

10-year manufacturer parts warranty.
Lifetime heat exchanger warranty.
2-year labor warranty.
