HVAC REPLACEMENT PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner is not cooling properly and system has experienced repeated breakdowns.

Existing equipment:
- 14-year-old split-system heat pump
- Existing outdoor unit: 3.5 tons
- Existing indoor air handler: matched 3.5-ton system
- Existing equipment model numbers documented
- Existing refrigerant system uses discontinued equipment components

Diagnostic findings:
- Compressor electrically tested and found to have abnormal winding resistance.
- Compressor amp draw exceeds manufacturer specifications.
- Compressor is mechanically noisy during operation.
- Refrigerant pressures and temperatures were measured and indicate poor compressor performance.
- Refrigerant charge was verified before condemning the compressor.
- Indoor airflow and blower operation were verified.
- Total external static pressure is within manufacturer limits.
- Evaporator and condenser coils were inspected and found reasonably clean.
- No major duct restriction was identified.
- Repair estimate for compressor replacement and associated refrigerant work: $7,800.
- Existing system has experienced multiple major repairs during the last 3 years.
- Due to system age, repair cost, equipment condition, and history of repeated failures, full replacement is recommended as the better long-term option.

Load and sizing evaluation:
- Manual J load calculation completed.
- Calculated cooling load: 41,000 BTUH.
- Calculated heating load: 43,500 BTUH.
- Proposed 3.5-ton heat pump capacity is appropriate for the calculated load.
- Existing duct system evaluated for proposed airflow requirements.
- Existing supply and return ductwork can support the proposed equipment airflow.
- No increase in equipment capacity is recommended.

Proposed replacement equipment:
- 3.5-ton variable-speed heat pump
- Exact outdoor model number documented in proposal
- Exact indoor air-handler model number documented in proposal
- Indoor and outdoor equipment are an AHRI-certified matched system.
- AHRI reference number documented.
- Manufacturer performance data reviewed for local design conditions.
- Equipment capacity is consistent with the Manual J calculation.
- Required airflow is compatible with the existing duct system.

Recommended work:
Replace existing heat pump system.

Scope of work:
- Remove existing outdoor heat pump
- Remove existing indoor air handler
- Install new matched 3.5-ton heat pump
- Install new matched indoor air handler
- Install new filter drier
- Flush or replace refrigerant piping as required by manufacturer
- Nitrogen purge while brazing
- Pressure test refrigerant circuit
- Evacuate system to manufacturer-required vacuum level
- Charge system according to manufacturer specifications
- Configure blower airflow
- Verify total external static pressure
- Verify refrigerant operation
- Verify heating and cooling operation
- Verify auxiliary heat operation
- Complete startup and commissioning

Total replacement price: $18,500

Parts and labor included.

10-year manufacturer parts warranty.
10-year compressor warranty.
2-year labor warranty.
