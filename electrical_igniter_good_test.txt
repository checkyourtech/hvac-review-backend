HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Gas furnace will not heat. Furnace completes the inducer and pressure-switch proving sequence, but the burners do not ignite.

Diagnosis:
Failed hot surface igniter.

Diagnostic findings:
- Furnace sequence of operation verified through the ignition stage.
- Draft inducer operates normally.
- Pressure switch closes and proves draft.
- Control board sends 120 volts to the hot surface igniter during the ignition sequence.
- Igniter does not glow when energized.
- Igniter resistance tested open / out of range.
- Igniter visually inspected and found cracked.
- Igniter wiring and connections inspected and found secure.
- No upstream safety or control condition was found preventing ignition.

Recommended repair:
Replace failed hot surface igniter.

Scope of work:
- Remove failed igniter
- Install replacement igniter
- Verify wiring and electrical connections
- Verify proper igniter operation
- Verify burner ignition
- Operate furnace through a complete heating cycle
- Confirm normal furnace operation
