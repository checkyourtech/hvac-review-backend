HVAC REPAIR PROPOSAL

Customer complaint:
Air conditioner runs but shuts off on high pressure after several minutes.

Diagnosis:
Condenser fan motor is bad.

Diagnostic findings:
- Outdoor fan is not running.
- Condenser coil gets very hot.
- High-pressure switch opens after several minutes.
- No documented voltage measurement at the condenser fan motor.
- No documented capacitor test.
- No documented amp draw.
- No documented winding resistance test.
- No documented motor-to-ground test.
- No documented check for a seized fan blade or mechanical obstruction.
- No documented control-board or contactor output verification.

Recommended repair:
Replace condenser fan motor.

Scope of work:
- Remove condenser fan motor
- Install replacement condenser fan motor
- Test system operation

Parts and labor included.
